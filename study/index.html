We are studying the tools people use to trade on Polymarket. For each tool we want to know two things: does it use an AI language model to predict market probabilities, and does it place trades by itself? A prediction market lets people bet on whether something will happen — an election result, a match, a price. Polymarket is the largest one. Every question is a market, and every market has a price between 0 and 1: that price is the crowd's probability that the event happens.

Many independent tools are built on top of Polymarket — dashboards, wallets, trading bots, copy-trading services, AI forecasters. Each card shows one such product through its own website, captured for this study, so you never leave this page. Page 2 of 3 · products with an AI feature

1. AI Does a large language model predict the market's probability?

The product uses a large language model — GPT, ChatGPT, Claude, OpenClaw, or a comparable AI language model — to predict the probability of a market outcome.

Every Polymarket market has a price, and that price is the market's implied probability. A product is AI when a language model forms its own estimate of that probability — the gap between its estimate and the market price is the predicted opportunity it offers the trader.

In practice this is a product that asks a language model to read the news and say how likely an outcome is — then shows you that number, or the gap between it and the market price, as a reason to trade.

Page 3 of 3 · products that act as an agent

2. Agent Does the product execute trades automatically?

The product places trades by itself, without the user entering each order — automated trading strategies, bots, rule-based execution, and copy trading.

The test is who presses the button. If the product executes on the trader's behalf once it is set up, it is an agent. If a person must place every order themselves, it is not.

What an agent looks like

Automated strategy — you set a rule such as “buy YES when the price falls below 0.30”; the product executes the moment the rule fires.
Trading bot — runs around the clock, opening and closing positions on its own.
Copy trading — you choose a trader or wallet; the product mirrors every trade they make, in your account, as they make it.
Rule-based execution — a stop-loss or take-profit that sells automatically at a level you set.
Not an agent — a dashboard, alert or signal that you still have to act on by hand.
